What does Research Collaboration mean?

You are not handed a template. We work through the project with you, from topic to analysis, so the work reflects your study and is ready for your supervisor.

Which package should I pick?

Pick Foundation if you only need the complete work delivered. Pick Refined if you want corrections effected after feedback. Pick Distinction if you want calls, voice notes and full defense preparation.

Is printing included?

Printing is not included in any package.
